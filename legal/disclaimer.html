KYVO is not medical advice

KYVO provides general fitness and nutrition information to help healthy adults train and eat more consistently. It is not medical, dietetic, physiotherapy or other professional health advice, and it does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition. KYVO is not a substitute for advice from your doctor or another qualified health professional.

All targets are estimates

Your calorie, protein, carbohydrate and fat targets, and your workouts, are generated from the information you enter (such as age range, height, weight, activity level and goal) using general formulas. They are estimates only. Everyone's body is different, and your actual needs may be higher or lower. Food logs and nutrition information may also be incomplete or inaccurate.

Check with a health professional first

Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise program or changing your diet, and consider seeing an Accredited Practising Dietitian for personalised nutrition advice. This is especially important if you:

  • are pregnant, may be pregnant, are breastfeeding or have recently given birth;
  • have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, kidney disease or any other medical condition;
  • take medication that may be affected by diet or exercise;
  • have an injury, joint problem or chronic pain;
  • have, or have had, an eating disorder or a difficult relationship with food or exercise;
  • have food allergies or intolerances (always check ingredient labels; KYVO cannot guarantee meals are free of allergens);
  • have not exercised in a long time.

Not for under 18s

KYVO is only for adults aged 18 and over. Its calorie and training estimates are not designed for children or teenagers, whose needs differ.

Eating disorders support

If tracking food, weight or body measurements makes you feel anxious or unwell, please stop and speak to a health professional. You can contact the Butterfly Foundation National Helpline on 1800 33 4673 (1800 ED HOPE) for support. [Confirm details before launch.]

Stop if something feels wrong

Stop exercising immediately and seek medical help if you feel chest pain, pressure or tightness, severe shortness of breath, dizziness or faintness, or sharp or unusual pain. Do not push through pain. In an emergency, call 000.

Use correct technique, warm up, choose weights you can control, and use equipment safely. If you are unsure how to do an exercise, ask a qualified exercise professional.

No guaranteed results

Results depend on many factors, including your genetics, health, sleep, stress, consistency and how closely you follow a plan. KYVO does not promise or guarantee any particular result, such as a specific amount of weight loss or muscle gain, within any timeframe.

Your responsibility

You choose whether and how to follow any plan in KYVO. Use your judgement, listen to your body, and follow the advice of your health professionals. Nothing in this disclaimer limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
