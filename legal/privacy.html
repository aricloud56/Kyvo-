1. Who this policy applies to

The Service is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will close it and delete the associated information.

2. What information we collect

Account information

  • Email address and password (we store passwords only in a hashed form; we cannot see your password).

Onboarding and profile information

  • Age range, height, weight, fitness goal, training experience, available equipment, preferred training days and activity level.
  • Diet preferences and any allergies or food exclusions you choose to tell us about (optional).
  • Sex, if you choose to provide it, to improve calorie estimates (optional).

Information you log

  • Workouts you complete, including exercises, sets, reps and weights.
  • Food and meals you log.
  • Body weight and body measurements.
  • Progress photos, if you choose to upload them (optional).
  • Daily Plan checklist activity.

Subscription and payment information

  • Your subscription plan, status and billing history. Card details are collected and processed directly by our payment provider, Stripe. We do not store your full card number.

Usage and technical information

  • Product events such as sign-up, onboarding completion, first workout logged, first meal logged and subscription start.
  • Campaign information from the link you used to reach us (for example, UTM parameters showing that you came from a TikTok campaign).
  • Device and browser type, IP address, and basic log data needed to run and secure the Service.

3. Health information is sensitive information

Some of the information you give us, such as your weight, height, body measurements, progress photos, workouts, food logs, allergies and information that may indicate a health condition, is likely to be health information. Under the Privacy Act, health information is a type of sensitive information and receives extra protection.

  • Consent: We only collect health information with your consent. We ask for this consent clearly during sign-up and onboarding, before you enter any health information. You can choose not to provide optional information (such as sex, allergies or progress photos), although some features may then be less accurate or unavailable.
  • Purpose: We use health information only to provide the Service to you: generating your Daily Plan, calculating estimated calorie and macronutrient (protein, carbohydrate, fat) targets, showing your progress, and letting you export your data. We do not use it for advertising.
  • No advertising use: We never send health, body, food or workout information to TikTok or any other advertising platform.
  • Withdrawing consent: You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the relevant information or deleting your account (see section 10). Withdrawing consent means we may no longer be able to provide some or all of the Service.

4. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To generate your personalised Daily Plan, workouts, meal suggestions and nutrition targets.
  • To store and display your logs, measurements and progress photos.
  • To process subscriptions and payments, and manage your billing.
  • To send you service messages, such as account, security, receipt and renewal reminder emails.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how people use the Service and which marketing campaigns bring people to KYVO, so we can improve the product.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent fraud and misuse, and fix problems.
  • To meet our legal obligations (for example, tax record-keeping).

We will only send you marketing emails if you have agreed to receive them, and every marketing email will include a working unsubscribe option. [Confirm Spam Act 2003 compliance.]

5. Cookies, analytics and the TikTok Pixel

  • Essential cookies and storage: We use cookies or similar storage that are needed to keep you signed in and make the Service work. These cannot be switched off.
  • First-party product analytics: We record a small set of product events (sign-up, onboarding completion, first workout, first meal log, subscription) and the campaign link you arrived from. This analytics data is stored in our own database and is not shared with advertising platforms.
  • TikTok Pixel (optional): If, and only if, you consent through our cookie/consent banner, we load the TikTok Pixel so we can measure whether our TikTok advertising works. The Pixel may receive information such as the fact that you visited a page or signed up, along with device and browser information. We never send health, body, food or workout information to TikTok. If you do not consent, the Pixel is not loaded. You can change your choice at any time in [Settings > Privacy / cookie settings link].

TikTok handles information it receives in line with its own privacy policy.

8. How we store and protect your information

  • Information is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase.
  • Progress photos are stored in private storage and are only accessible to you through your account. They are not public and are not shared.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). [Confirm encryption at rest with provider.]
  • Access to personal information is restricted to people who need it to operate and support the Service.
  • Passwords are stored using one-way hashing.

No system is completely secure. If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.
