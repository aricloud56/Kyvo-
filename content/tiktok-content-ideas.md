## Compliance (read before filming or posting)

- **No before/after or transformation claims.** Do not show before/after photos, promise weight loss or muscle gain, or state results in a timeframe.
- **No fabricated statistics or testimonials.** Don't quote numbers you can't source. Don't script fake reviews or present actors as real customers.
- **Targets are estimates; not medical advice.** Where calorie or macro targets appear, say they are estimates. Link the Fitness and Nutrition Disclaimer from landing pages.
- **No health or medical claims.** Don't claim KYVO treats, prevents or cures any condition (TGA considerations).
- **Disclose paid partnerships.** Any creator paid or gifted to post must use TikTok's branded content/paid partnership toggle and a clear disclosure (e.g. "#ad" / "Paid partnership with KYVO") in line with AANA and ACCC influencer guidance.
- **Demo data only.** Use demo accounts in screen recordings; never show a real user's health, body, food or workout data.
- **Pricing accuracy.** If prices are mentioned, they must match the current site (Premium A$9.99/month or A$79.99/year) and state that the subscription auto-renews.
- **Safety.** Show safe technique, avoid extreme dieting messages, and include "stop if you feel pain" where relevant.
- **Audience.** KYVO is for adults 18+. Don't target content at under-18s.
- **Licensing.** Use only TikTok commercial-licensed music for brand accounts; get releases from anyone filmed.
